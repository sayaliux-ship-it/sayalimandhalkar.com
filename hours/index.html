01 — The project

A launch campaign built the way beauty teams actually ship one: one idea, one system, proven across every commerce surface.

HOURS is an invented premium skincare brand built around skin chronobiology. For its hero product — an overnight recovery mask named after the hour skin’s repair activity peaks — I developed the campaign idea, the visual system, and the full set of digital commerce executions.

02 — The brief

Launch a hero night mask in a category where every night cream promises the same eight hours. Make the overnight story feel ownable, and make one campaign system carry it from a DTC hero to a retailer product tile without falling apart.

03 — The idea

While you’re off the clock,
your skin clocks in.

Skin’s own repair activity peaks in the middle of the night — the hours we only ever talk about as lost sleep. The Night Shift reframes 3AM as a place where quiet work gets done. The product is named after that hour, and the whole campaign lives inside it.

04 — The system

One device carries the campaign: a thin line of dawn light cutting across deep ink.

It appears in the photography as real light, on the packaging as a printed horizon at the base of the jar, and in layouts as a gradient rule. Wherever an asset shrinks to a thumbnail, the line is the last thing to survive — so recognition holds at any size.
