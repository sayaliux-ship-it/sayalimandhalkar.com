03 — Questions

Rewards, explained.

What is the Insider programme?

It rewards you for shopping, engaging with our community and staying consistent with your routine. Earn points for purchases, reviews, refill subscriptions and more, then redeem them against future orders.

How do points work?

You earn 1 point for every $1 spent. 500 points converts to a $10 reward, applied at checkout.

Can I earn points on refills?

Yes — we reward sustainable habits. You earn 100 points when you buy your first refill. Limit one refill reward per customer.

What do subscriptions earn?

125 points when you start a Subscribe & Save order, plus 15% off every delivery. When your second order ships you receive a further 150 points and a free deluxe gift.

How does Refer a Friend work?

Your friend gets $20 off their first order over $50. You receive 1,000 points once their purchase completes.

What is the Pact recycling reward?

We partner with Pact Collective to reduce beauty waste. Return five empties — full-size or refill packaging — and we send you a code for a free refill.

Do my points expire?

Yes. Points expire 12 months from the date they are earned.
