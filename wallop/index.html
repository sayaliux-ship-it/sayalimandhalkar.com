The idea

Hot sauce brands sell heat like a dare. WALLOP sells the fix for boring food.

So the campaign never shows peppers, flames or skulls — it shows ordinary meals at the exact moment they stop being ordinary. Everything is shot like a snapshot, not an ad: direct flash, real kitchens, greasy pans. The wallop is the only art direction.

The system

One condensed word, one splat, three colors. Loud enough to read across a diner table — or a 60-pixel thumbnail.
