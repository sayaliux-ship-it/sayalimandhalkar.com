EXTENSION. On the marketing site these four are interchangeable decoration. A daily app cannot afford that — a child needs to know at a glance whether a card is a habit, a reward, sleep, or their medication. Fixing the jobs buys that legibility without adding a single color. The clinical/gamified split is the important one: medication never appears in sun or mint.
