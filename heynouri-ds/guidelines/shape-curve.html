"On a mission to bend the obesity curve down" is the company's mission statement, and the curve is not a metaphor in the design — it is a literal, repeated asset: paired ellipse-up-xl / ellipse-down-xl files, a curve framing the doctor portrait, photos arranged on an oval path, 90px bubbles, and pill radius on 149 elements. There are no sharp diagonals, triangles or hard angles anywhere in this system. In the app this means progress rings rather than bars, and arc charts rather than columns.
